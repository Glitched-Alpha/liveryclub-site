+Ask Livery

Ask anything about your car, in plain English

Something not right? A warning light, a noise, an MOT advisory you don't understand. Ask Livery answers using your car's own records, and tells you how urgent it is.

  • Knows your car's MOT, service and cost history
  • Reads photos, invoices and PDFs
  • Clear urgency: fine, get it checked soon, or stop

+MOT advisory tracker

Your MOT advisories, turned into a to-do list

Advisories are easy to forget until they become next year's fail. Livery lists them from your last MOT and keeps track until they're sorted.

  • Mark each one fixed or not needed
  • See what's worth sorting before the next MOT
  • Ask Livery about any advisory in one tap

+Receipt scanning

Snap a receipt and the details fill themselves in

Take a photo of a fuel, parts or garage receipt and Livery reads the amount, date and where it was from, so logging costs takes seconds.

  • Works for fuel, parts, servicing and more
  • Keeps the receipt with the cost
  • Fuel fill-ups feed straight into your MPG

+Fuel economy

Know your real-world MPG

Log your fill-ups and Livery works out the MPG you're actually getting, what each mile costs in fuel and your average price per litre.

  • Average and last-tank MPG
  • Fuel cost per mile and per litre
  • Spot a drop in economy early

+Business mileage

Business miles, logged and ready to claim

Log work trips as you go and Livery totals them by tax year at HMRC's approved rates, ready for your expenses or self assessment.

  • 55p a mile for the first 10,000, then 25p
  • This and last tax year side by side
  • Export when you need it

+Clean air zones

Check London's ULEZ and every UK clean air zone

See whether your car is charged in London's ULEZ and the other UK clean air and low emission zones, worked out from your car's details.

  • London, Birmingham, Bristol, Scotland and more
  • Clear charge or no-charge for each zone
  • Checked for every car in your garage

+Shared garages

Share a car with family or a partner

Everyone who drives the car sees the same dates, history and reminders, and can add work, fuel and documents if you let them.

  • Invite by email
  • Choose can edit or view only
  • One history, kept by everyone

Good to know

Where do I get Plus?

Here on the web. Sign in to Livery, choose monthly or yearly and pay securely with Stripe. Plus switches on straight away, on the web and in the app.

Can I cancel?

Whenever you like, in Livery. Plus stays on until the end of the period you've paid for and then stops. Your cars and records stay in your garage.

What happens to my data if I stop?

Nothing is deleted. You go back to the free plan, and everything you've added is still there.

Already subscribed?

Sign in on your phone with the same account and Plus is already there. Nothing to restore.
