Questions.

When can I start using Livery?

Soon. We're opening in batches, starting with people on the waitlist. Join with your email and we'll send your invite as soon as there's a place for you.

Is Livery free?

Yes, Livery will be free to use. Later we may add optional extras for enthusiasts, and we'll always tell you before anything changes.

What will you do with my email?

Only email you about early access and the launch. We never sell it, and you can unsubscribe from any email at any time.

Which cars can I add?

Any car registered in the UK, by its plate. You can also add any car by entering its details yourself.

Where is my data kept?

On secure servers in London, encrypted in transit and at rest. Only you can see your garage.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. Delete it from your account settings and your garage, records, photos and documents are erased straight away. You can download a copy first.
