Install Livery on your phone or computer.

Livery runs in your browser, and you can add it to your home screen, taskbar or Dock so it opens in its own window, just like an app. Sign in once and you'll stay signed in.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open app.liveryclub.com in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow). On newer versions of iOS, tap ••• first, then Share.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. Livery now opens from its icon on your home screen.

Android

  1. Open app.liveryclub.com in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top corner.
  3. Tap Add to home screen, then Install.
  4. Livery appears on your home screen and in your app list. On Samsung Internet, use Menu → Add page to → Home screen.

Windows

  1. Open app.liveryclub.com in Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome.
  2. Click the Install icon at the right of the address bar. Or use ••• → Apps → Install this site as an app in Edge, or ⋮ → Cast, save and share → Install page as app in Chrome.
  3. Click Install. Livery opens in its own window and is added to the Start menu.
  4. To keep it handy, right-click Livery in the taskbar and choose Pin to taskbar.

Mac

  1. Safari (macOS Sonoma or later): open app.liveryclub.com, choose File → Add to Dock, then click Add. Livery appears in your Dock straight away.
  2. Chrome: click the Install icon in the address bar, or ⋮ → Cast, save and share → Install page as app, then Install.
  3. To keep the Chrome version in your Dock, right-click its Dock icon while it's open and choose Options → Keep in Dock.
  4. You'll also find Livery in Launchpad and in your Applications folder.

Questions.

When can I start using Livery?

Today. Livery is available at app.liveryclub.com: create a free account and add your car by its registration. It runs in the browser on any phone or computer and can be installed like an app. Native apps for iPhone and Android are coming soon to the App Store and Google Play, with your garage already inside.

Is Livery free?

Yes. Livery Free covers up to two cars with MOT and tax lookups, key date countdowns, reminders, service history, mods, running costs and documents, for as long as you like. Livery Plus adds receipt scanning, fuel and MPG tracking, the MOT advisory tracker, clean air zone checks, a business mileage log and shared garages, unlimited cars and Ask Livery, your AI car helper, for £5.99 a month or £49.99 a year (£39.99 a year with the launch offer until 31 January).

What's the launch offer?

Until the end of 31 January, yearly Plus is £39.99 (£3.33 a month) instead of £49.99, and you keep that price for as long as you remain subscribed. After that, yearly goes back to £49.99. Monthly is £5.99. If you joined the waitlist before launch, the £39.99 price stays open to you after January too.

Can I cancel Plus?

Yes, whenever you like, in the app. Plus stays on until the end of the period you've paid for and then stops renewing. Plus starts the moment you subscribe, so payments aren't refundable. Your cars and records stay in your garage.

What happens to my data?

It's used to run your garage and nothing else. Your garage is private to you, stored in the UK, and we never sell your data. Our Privacy Policy has the details.

Which cars can I add?

Any car registered in the UK, by its plate. You can also add any car by entering its details yourself.

Where is my data kept?

On secure servers in London, encrypted in transit and at rest. Only you can see your garage.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. Delete it from your account settings and your garage, records, photos and documents are erased straight away. You can download a copy first.
